Privacy Policy

Photo Sweep · Last updated: November 17, 2026

Photo Sweep helps you sort through the photos on your iPhone one at a time and delete the ones you don't want. It is built so that your photos never leave your device.

1. What we collect

We do not collect any personal data. Photo Sweep has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting, and it does not include any third-party SDK. The app does not connect to any server run by us or by a third party.

2. Your photos

  • Photo Sweep asks for access to your photo library so it can show, sort, compress and delete photos and videos. You choose the level of access in iOS.
  • All analysis (similar photos, screenshots, text and QR codes) runs on your device. Photos and analysis results are not uploaded anywhere.
  • Nothing is deleted or replaced without your confirmation. Deleted photos go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app.
  • Your choices (keep, delete, skip) are stored only on your device.

3. Apple services the app uses

  • Place names: to show where a photo was taken, the photo's location coordinates are sent to Apple's geocoding service. The photo itself is not sent.
  • iCloud Photos: if your originals are stored in iCloud, iOS downloads them from Apple when needed (for example, to compress a video).
  • iCloud key-value storage and Keychain: the number of photos deleted with the free version today and this month is kept in your own iCloud and Keychain, so the free limit stays the same after reinstalling. It contains only dates and counts.
  • Notifications: new-photo reminders are scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent from a server.

4. Purchases

Subscriptions and the one-time purchase are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. You can manage or cancel a subscription in iOS Settings.

5. Children

Photo Sweep does not collect data from anyone, including children.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: liu.qiujun@icloud.com

隐私政策

相册清理 · 最后更新：2026 年 11 月 17 日

相册清理帮你一张一张整理 iPhone 上的照片，删掉不需要的。App 的设计保证你的照片不会离开手机。

1. 我们收集什么

我们不收集任何个人数据。相册清理没有账号，不做统计、不放广告、不做崩溃上报，也不接入任何第三方 SDK。App 不连接我们或任何第三方的服务器。

2. 你的照片

  • 相册清理需要访问你的照片图库，用来显示、整理、压缩和删除照片与视频。访问范围由你在 iOS 中选择。
  • 所有分析（相似照片、截图、文字和二维码识别）都在手机上完成，照片和分析结果不会上传到任何地方。
  • 没有你的确认，App 不会删除或替换任何照片。删除的照片会先进入「照片」App 的「最近删除」。
  • 你的选择（留、删、跳过）只保存在手机上。

3. App 使用的苹果服务

  • 地名：为了显示照片的拍摄地点，照片的经纬度会发送给苹果的地名查询服务，照片本身不会发送。
  • iCloud 照片：原图存在 iCloud 上时，iOS 会在需要时（例如压缩视频）从苹果下载原图。
  • iCloud 键值存储和钥匙串：免费版当天和当月已删除的张数，保存在你自己的 iCloud 和钥匙串里，删掉 App 重装后免费额度不变。其中只有日期和张数。
  • 通知：新照片提醒在手机上安排，不经过任何服务器。

4. 购买

订阅和买断完全由苹果通过 App Store 处理，我们收不到你的付款信息。你可以在 iOS「设置」中管理或取消订阅。

5. 儿童

相册清理不收集任何人的数据，包括儿童。

6. 变更

政策有变化时，我们会更新本页和上面的日期。

7. 联系我们

关于本政策的问题：liu.qiujun@icloud.com
